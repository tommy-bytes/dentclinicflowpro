Privacy Policy

The clinic's data stays in the clinic's own iCloud account and on its own devices. The developer never receives it, never sees it and never stores it.

Version 1.0 · effective 3 September 2026

1. In short

  • The app sends nothing to any server operated by the developer. There is no such server.
  • All clinic data — patients, staff, appointments, attendance — is written on the device and synchronised through the private CloudKit database in the user's own iCloud account.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs and no advertising identifier.
  • Subscription payment is handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no card number and no payment details.

2. Who the controller is

The clinic using the app is the data controller for the patient and staff data it enters. The clinic decides what it records, how long it keeps it, and who may see it through the app's roles.

MSCS di Stefan E. supplies the software. It is not a processor of the clinic's data, because it never receives it: the data moves only between the clinic's devices and the clinic's own iCloud account, through Apple's infrastructure.

3. What the app processes, and where it goes

CategoryExamplesStored where
Patient data name, phone, email, date of birth (optional), preferred language, clinic notes device + the clinic's private CloudKit
Staff data name, role, specialty, contact, shifts, attendance, leave device + the clinic's private CloudKit
Appointments time, duration, doctor, room, type, reason, internal notes device + the clinic's private CloudKit
Apple identifier the stable user identifier returned by Sign in with Apple the device's Keychain
Audit log who, what, when, previous and new value, device type device + the clinic's private CloudKit

The app has no account of its own. People sign in with Sign in with Apple, and only the stable user identifier is kept. The name and email Apple offers on first sign-in are used to pre-fill the clinic setup form and are not stored if that form is not completed.

4. Location

Location is read only at the moment somebody clocks in or out, and only if the clinic has turned location verification on. The app does not track location in the background and does not monitor movement.

What is kept from the reading is the verification outcome ("at the clinic", "away from the clinic", "location access off", "too imprecise") and the distance in metres from the centre of the clinic's area. Coordinates are not stored.

5. Camera and biometrics

The camera is used only to read a QR code — a clinic invitation, or the attendance code shown at reception. No photograph or image is saved.

Identity at clock-in is confirmed through Face ID or Touch ID using the system's LocalAuthentication service. The app never receives or stores biometric data; the system answers only yes or no.

6. Notifications

Notifications about appointments, shifts and leave are generated locally on the device. The app uses CloudKit's silent notifications only to learn that data changed on another device belonging to the same clinic. No marketing notifications are sent.

In settings, a clinic can hide patient names from previews, so a lock screen shows "Upcoming appointment at 10:30" rather than the person's name.

7. Payment

The subscription is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether the subscription is active. The developer does not receive card details, billing address or the buyer's identity — only the aggregated sales reports Apple provides.

8. How long data is kept

Data stays for as long as the clinic keeps it. Deleting a patient removes them from the database; their appointments remain in the clinic's operational history without their name. Security-relevant audit entries are not deleted automatically.

Deleting the app removes the local copy. Data in the clinic's iCloud account is removed from that account's own iCloud settings.

9. Data subject rights

For patient and staff data, requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection go to the clinic as controller. The developer cannot answer them, because it does not hold the data and has no technical means of reaching it.

For anything about the app itself, write to stefan@support-remote.org.

10. Children

The app is for clinic staff, not the general public, and is not directed at children. A clinic may record a minor as a patient on the same basis as in any other clinic record, under its own responsibility.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the version and effective date at the top of the page are updated. Earlier versions remain in the public history of this site's repository.
